How often each pair was live on the winning turn in 3,000 goldfish games: Hope+Cradle 56% · Magus+Cradle 55% · Hope+Nykthos 30% · Magus+Nykthos 27% · Quirion+Selvala 16% · Quirion+Cub 12% · Scryb+Selvala 12% · Quirion+Bloom 11%.

Special case: Hope Tender + Shang-Chi live 26%

Shang-Chi taps for two mana that only pays for creature abilities. Hope's untap is one, and so is Sisay's. So Ashaya + Hope + Shang-Chi + Sisay gives unlimited Sisay activations: fetch Selvala, and the same engine now makes real mana. Shang-Chi also gives haste to abilities, so neither Hope nor Sisay needs to wait a turn.

Engine B · Emiel

Emiel blinks the untapper instead

How to animate Cradle

  • Badgermole Cub earthbend: gives haste, and Cub adds G when the Cradle creature taps. live 17%
  • Kamahl {G}: becomes a 1/1 until end of turn.
  • Animate Land, Verdant Touch: permanent, but Cradle must have been in play since your turn began.

Why Flash is the key

  • Flash can untap Sisay each pass too: unlimited legends without Ashaya.
  • Flash plus Satyr or Arbor Elf also works on Nykthos at devotion 6+.
  • Emiel + Flash + Cradle is live in about 1 in 6 wins, and is the deck's whole plan when Ashaya is gone.
The finish

Infinite mana into Sisay into Kamahl

Cheat sheet

What to look for at the table

  1. Ashaya in play or fetchable? Then any one untapper (Hope, Magus, Quirion, Scryb) plus any one source from the matrix. Cradle with 2+ creatures is the easiest.
  2. Hope or Magus cast this turn? You need Concordant Crossroads, Elixir, Shang-Chi or Greaves. Quirion and Scryb don't care.
  3. No Ashaya? Emiel + Flash + Fanatic is the cleanest. Otherwise Emiel + Flash + an untapped Cradle route with 4 creatures.
  4. Sisay untapped? Every engine can untap or re-buy her, so once any loop runs you own every legend: Kamahl, Selvala, Cradle, Nykthos, Emiel, Flash, Shang-Chi.
  5. Kamahl countered or exiled? Finale of Devastation for X ≥ 10 is the only other finisher in the list.
  6. Never infinite: Formidable Speaker, Thousand-Year Elixir, Arbor Elf, Voyaging Satyr and Marvin alone. They tap themselves to untap one thing, so there is never a spare untap. Elixir and Formidable Speaker are haste and value, not engines.
